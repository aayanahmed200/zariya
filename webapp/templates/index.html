About your chat data

Your messages are sent to Vercel AI Gateway and the model provider to generate replies. The selected provider does not advertise zero-data-retention or a no-training guarantee. Voice typing uses your browser's speech-recognition service, whose audio handling depends on that provider. Avoid entering passwords, private records or other sensitive information.

The selected model is listed at $0 per token, but it is a shared service and can be rate-limited or temporarily unavailable. Zariya then falls back to its smaller built-in knowledge mode.
